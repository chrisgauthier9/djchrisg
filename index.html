About

Whatever you want to hear, played right.

Weddings, private parties, corporate events – the format changes, the job doesn't. Get the right song to the right moment, keep the levels clean, and never leave dead air while people wait to dance.

Hand me a playlist, a must-play list, or a do-not-play list and I'll follow it. Want me to just read the room instead? I do that too.

What to hand me

  • Your playlist – Spotify, Apple Music, or a plain list
  • Ceremony & first dance songs, if it's a wedding
  • Must-plays and do-not-plays
  • Timeline from your planner or venue
